# Free AI Models (Claude/OpenAI Alternatives)
Zero/low cost, high-limit AI models for development.

## Options
• **Google Gemini**  
  Free tier: 15 RPM, 1k requests/day  
  Sign up: https://ai.google.dev/  
  API docs: https://ai.google.dev/docs

• **DeepSeek**  
  Free API: 500 requests/day, no credit card required  
  Sign up: https://platform.deepseek.com/

• **Groq**  
  Free tier: 14k tokens/day, ultra-fast inference  
  Supports: Llama 3, Mixtral, Gemma  
  Sign up: https://console.groq.com/

• **Mistral AI**  
  Free tier for Mixtral 8x7B, Mistral 7B  
  Sign up: https://mistral.ai/

• **Hugging Face Inference API**  
  Free for open-source models (Llama, Falcon, etc.)  
  Sign up: https://huggingface.co/inference-api

• **OpenRouter Free Models**  
  Mix of free models (Gemini Flash, Mixtral, etc.)  
  List: https://openrouter.ai/docs/models/free

• **Meta Llama 3**  
  Available via Hugging Face (free) or Replicate (trial credits)  
  Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/meta-llama
